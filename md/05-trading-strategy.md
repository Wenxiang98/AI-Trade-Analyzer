## Chosen Strategy: DCA + Disciplined Swing

### Primary: Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) into VOO/SPY
- **Method:** Buy a fixed RM amount every month on a set date (e.g. 1st of month)
- **Target:** VOO (80%) + QQQ optional (20%)
- **Monthly amount:** RM 300–500
- **Hold period:** 5–10+ years minimum
- **Exit trigger:** Only sell for real life goal (house, emergency) — not market fear

### Secondary: Disciplined Swing Trading (Bursa Malaysia)
- **Universe:** Bursa Malaysia stocks (banks, semis, REITs, consumer)
- **Risk per trade:** 2% of capital maximum = RM 20 on RM 1,000 account
- **Minimum R:R:** 1:2 (for every RM 1 risked, target RM 2 gain)
- **Stop loss:** Always set before entry — no exceptions
- **Hold period:** Days to weeks (swing, not day trade)

## The Hard Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Never risk more than 2% per trade.** On RM 1,000 = RM 20 max loss per trade.
2. **Always set stop loss before entering.** No stop = no trade.
3. **Never add to a losing position** to "average down" without a new thesis.
4. **Minimum R:R of 1:2.** If the trade doesn't offer it, skip it.
5. **Don't chase.** If a stock ran 10%+ already, wait for the next setup.
6. **DCA is mechanical.** Buy on schedule, not on mood or market news.
7. **When market drops 20%+ — buy more VOO.** Crashes are sales.
8. **No leverage, no margin, no warrants** until at least 1 year of profitable trading.

## Behavioral Rules (Fighting Yourself)

| Temptation | Rule |
|---|---|
| "This stock is going to moon, I need to buy NOW" | Wait 1 day. If still valid, enter with proper sizing. |
| "I'm down 15%, I'll hold until it recovers" | Respect the stop loss. Cut it. |
| "I already lost on this, let me make it back fast" | Do not revenge trade. Take a day off. |
| "The market dropped, I should sell everything" | Do nothing. This is why you sized small. |
| "This hot tip from a friend looks great" | Run it through the analyzer. No tip trades without a plan. |
| "Let me just check prices 20 times today" | Check portfolio once a day max. |
